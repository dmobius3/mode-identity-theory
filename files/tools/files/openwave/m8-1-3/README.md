# 🏄‍♂️ M8.1.3: A Blind Run on the Standing-Wave Branches

M8.1.3 is a blind verification on OpenWave of what the [Standing-Wave Branches](/files/framework/files/dynamics/files/standing-wave-branches.md) carries, in five blocks, all five accepted when its filing merged as [#731](https://github.com/openwave-labs/openwave/pull/731) on 2026-11-16. Its files live in this folder, as [M8 on OpenWave](/files/tools/files/openwave/README.md) sets out.

## The question

On 2026-11-15 (UTC) MIT [asked](https://github.com/openwave-labs/openwave/discussions/512#discussioncomment-19623367) on #512 whether the maintainer would take the audit, whole or by block, as blind verification of what a paper already carries: the M8.1.x series, as [the answer of 2026-09-12](https://github.com/openwave-labs/openwave/discussions/512#discussioncomment-18415036) describes it. The blocks are A, the reduced problems; B, the small-amplitude data at the 21 orbits and points of Tables 4 and 5, the priority; C, the values beyond leading order; D, the interval certificates at the three outside orbits; and E, optional, the lowest level of the rigid slots. The question leaves out the paper's numerical study, which is the column's own adjudicated record; what M8.12, M8.1.2 and M8.10 reproduced blind; model checks that state no value the paper reports; and the proofs, which would be their own ask. No result in the paper rests on the code changed off M8.16.1's pin, on which MIT's own computation runs, outside M8.

**Note (2026-11-15), on the answer.** The maintainer [answered](https://github.com/openwave-labs/openwave/discussions/512#discussioncomment-19632863) on 2026-11-15: the audit may file as M8.1.3, a BACKLOG row with its task document in the pull request that carries it, and each block is accepted or declined in that pull request's thread before it merges. The answer admits the filing and nothing more: it accepts no block and no workload, nothing is pending until the task document files, and whether and when it files is MIT's. Three blocks reach what the column's closed record took from MIT's pages: B, the small-amplitude types, which pick out the branches born elliptic that M8.15 to M8.19 followed; C, Proposition 4.7, which is Proposition N of the [Soft-Slot Branches](/files/framework/files/working/files/soft-slot-branches.md), whose next-order terms M8.16.1 scored its instrument against; and D, the three outside orbits, which M8.12's task document leaves outside its claim. The third, O₃, is T4, whose rows M8.15 and M8.16.1 to M8.19 carry. A is B's input, and E stands apart. The answer adds five points to the shape:
- R5 computed from its own coefficients, with Lemma 3.1(c)'s relation to R4 scored as a claim, or R5's rows labelled as following from the lemma and not verified;
- for each kind of value in Block C, whether a room evaluates the paper's closed form or computes the value from its definition, a pass on a closed form recording only that the form gives the stated value;
- each quantity marked as an input, with the task and record it comes from, or as a claim, among them the identification of Lemma 3.1(a)'s spin-6 factor with M8.12's `r̂₆`;
- a failed claim read first for its cause, and a claim that frozen text is defective reproduced by the maintainer with independent code before it is ratified; where a ratified defect touches a closed task, that task does not reopen and nothing reruns under any ID, and the defect is recorded in M8.1.3's own record, which names each closed task it touches and what that task rested on;
- the verdict's scope: the PDF pinned by its version DOI, [10.5281/zenodo.24224845](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24224845), and its hash, a later version outside it. A pass reads as M8.1.2's does, the frozen claims reproducing blind, and claims no theorem; no `MODELS.md` icon moves on the outcome, M8.7's gate stays as it is, and whether a cell's text records the outcome is decided after the adjudication.

The two `MODELS.md` criteria the thread is about, gated on field dynamics, stay open, as [answer 1](https://github.com/openwave-labs/openwave/discussions/512#discussioncomment-18222368) left them. The answer also sets conditions on the task document, among them that the claims file reaches the maintainer before the rooms open; the rooms never see it, Block D's boxes apart. This note records the answer before the task document is drafted, as the question said. The task document comes next, with its obligations table, for the maintainer's acceptance block by block in its thread.
