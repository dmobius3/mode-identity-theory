Geodesic Lune, Möbius Quotient
In the conic model, the leading realization, the carrier is a lune on the great S², unbent, whose opposite tips N and −N the antipodal map −I identifies. Joined at its tips, it is the Möbius band: one side, one edge, embedded in ℝP³ = S³/{±I}. The seam lands on its equator arc. The lune has constant curvature K = 1/R², widest at the equator and pinching to the tips. The twist is topological: the orientation reverses only through the joined tips, and the rest of the lune is orientable.
