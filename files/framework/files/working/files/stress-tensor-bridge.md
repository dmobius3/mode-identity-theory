**A structural observation, recorded as a hint and not a result.** With $`\Psi = \cos(t/2)`$ and $`S = \sin(t/2)`$ the budget identity is the harmonic energy identity: $`\dot\Psi = -S/2`$, so

```math
4\dot\Psi^2 + \Psi^2 = S^2 + \Psi^2 = 1.
```

A quadratic energy density of the standing wave is time-independent even though each share oscillates. So C1's staticity is natural for the energy density of any quadratic reading of the wave. It is not automatic for the pressure, which for a scalar reading is kinetic minus potential and oscillates with zero mean. Whether the physical $`T_{\mu\nu}`$ is such a quadratic reading is exactly the open dictionary; this observation constrains candidates, it does not select one.

**The trace form of C2 (2026-10-09).** On branches (a) and (c), where Einstein's equations hold on the exact static metric, the total source is everything on their right once the vacuum-like constant has been placed by the $`\Lambda_\text{static}`$ declaration below: moving a constant $`c`$ from the source into Λ shifts $`\rho`$ by $`-c`$, $`p`$ by $`+c`$, and $`\Lambda R^2`$ by $`8\pi G c R^2`$. On the exact static metric, $`G_{00}`$ is constant, $`G_{0i} = 0`$ and $`G_{ij} \propto g_{ij}`$, so the total is a perfect fluid at rest whatever its constituents, with $`8\pi G(\rho + p) = 2/R^2`$ and no momentum density. With $`T^\mu{}_\mu = 3p - \rho`$, C2 then reads

```math
\Lambda R^2 = \frac{3}{2} + \frac{3p - \rho}{2(\rho + p)} = \frac{3}{2} + 2\pi G R^2\, T^\mu{}_\mu ,
```

and since $`3(\rho + p) - T^\mu{}_\mu = 4\rho`$, the screen $`\Lambda R^2 < 3`$ is exactly $`\rho > 0`$, C2's own condition. Two rungs below the screen hold for stated classes, each bounding a total source made wholly of its class:

- **Rung 2:** $`\Lambda R^2 \le 2`$ for scalar fields with minimal coupling, a positive kinetic form and $`V \ge 0`$. The total has $`\rho - p = \tfrac23\lvert\nabla\varphi\rvert^2 + 2V \ge 0`$, summed over real components, so $`w \le 1`$.
- **Rung 3/2:** $`\Lambda R^2 \le 3/2`$ for scalar fields with any curvature coupling $`\xi`$, in bounded oscillation, with $`\varphi V' \le 4V`$. On shell $`T^\mu{}_\mu = \varphi V' - 4V + \tfrac12(6\xi - 1)\,\Box\varphi^2`$; the last term averages to zero over the closed section and a long time, and the static total equals its average. A mass and a quartic qualify. Free fields give $`\langle T^\mu{}_\mu\rangle = -m^2\langle\varphi^2\rangle`$, and 3/2 itself when massless.

The growth condition is needed. With minimal coupling, bounded oscillation and a nonnegative potential homogeneous of degree $`2k > 4`$, the virial theorem bounds $`\Lambda R^2`$ by $`2 - 1/k`$, with equality exactly when the fields carry no gradient energy: the rotating mode of an ordinary complex scalar reaches the bound, and a doublet of fields in the two-dimensional slot bundle approaches it at large amplitude. One such slot field alone carries momentum along a Hopf Killing field, which the static metric forbids. Both rungs need the zero of $`V`$ fixed with the class, and one positive-trace component outside the class can overturn them. (DERIVED.)

## III. The placement fork

Three branches, mutually exclusive, each a legitimate outcome. The corpus is currently implicit on which branch it occupies; the translation-layer language of the budget pages leans (b) without saying so. Deciding the branch is part of closing the bridge, and each branch has stated consequences.

- **The propagation theorem: proved,** and extended to every order below six, in [The Propagation Theorem](propagation-theorem.md). On $`S^3/2I`$, every real characteristic sheet of a linear differential operator equivariant under the lifted right action of $`SU(2)`$, with a real scalar principal symbol of order at most five, is a round cone, apart from the sheet $`\xi_0 = 0`$ at odd orders. At second order the cone fixes the conformal class of $`-c(t)\,dt^2 + h_\text{round}`$, and the scale of a metric representative needs a declared standard. For a strictly hyperbolic operator, rays follow geodesics of $`h_\text{round}`$, one per image, with the comoving transverse factor $`R\sin(\chi/R)`$; beyond the injectivity radius $`\pi R/10`$ a source can have several images. It closes only its class: matrix symbols, order six and above, broken homogeneity, nonlinear or nonlocal laws, laws without real principal type, and a separate effective manifold lie outside it.

Past that radius an assumption fails. It becomes a one-line theorem once the Jacobian convention is frozen.
- **The trace bound.** For a frozen class of total source on branches (a) and (c), the supremum of $`\Lambda R^2`$: a rung, rather than a pass or fail against 3. Classes are nested, beginning with scalar and twisted-bundle matter.
