- **1b. The realization (every reading).** Is the carrier the conic band $`M(W)`$? Under (A) it is the leading explicit singular band in $`\mathbb{RP}^3`$, and under (C) it is the pillar's own model. Under (A′) and (B′), if $`M(W)`$ is selected, Propositions 0.3 and 0.2 supply free families of bent embeddings, and nothing on this page selects among them. Under (B) the carrier is a band in $`S^3`$, and $`M(W)`$ is at most its spectral stand-in. Of §V's three requirements, the second, geodesic edge arcs, has no framework ground on this page: it is inherited from the regime of Tier 2's first computation. The third does not supply it: under the Friedrichs realization, near every $`M(W)`$, suitably chosen moves of one lap off its line within $`\mathbb{RP}^2`$, on an arc away from $`p_c`$, keep the exact $`2/R^2`$ (Proposition 4.2). Within Proposition 3.1's class line tension does supply it, at zero effective sheet tension, which is only MOTIVATED, and keeping that class's pinch is a restriction the seed motivates without deriving (INFERRED) ([The Carrier's Edge Regimes](carrier-edge-regimes.md)). - **1c. The boundary regime (every placed reading).**

An independently motivated edge action, support law or boundary Euler-Lagrange condition that makes the temporal edge dynamical, judged without requiring it to preserve $`M(W)`$, or an observable effect that needs the edge's locus to respond, would overturn the choice.
