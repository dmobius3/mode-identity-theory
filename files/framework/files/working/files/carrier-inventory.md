## What The Projective Carrier does not do

Apart from a paragraph in the Λ page's §III and the pointers to this page from the postulate bridge and the working index, it changes no other page's claims. The premise is adopted (§XI), so the pages below took scope notes or rewording, made separately. The research debts the adoption raises, rather than rewording, are in §XI's ledger under (A). The readings (A), (A′), (B), (B′) and (C) are §XI's. One entry, at the end, is independent of the ruling; the Λ page carries it. Since 2026-09-27 the working-layer pages below carry dated scope notes, except Cone Point Coherence, which the ruling leaves as it was. Since 2026-09-28 the public pages state the placement, the two seams and the two signs in place, with the formula rewritten (§XI, 1a); the Euclid card carries the change as a dated clarification.

**Where the carrier sits.** Until 2026-09-28 these put the Möbius surface in $`S^3`$ itself, and since then each says where it embeds, with the formula rewritten to put the arrow on the band, $`S^1 = \partial(\text{Möbius}),\ \text{Möbius} \hookrightarrow \mathbb{RP}^3 = S^3/\{\pm I\},\ \partial S^3 = \emptyset`$ (§XI, 1a):
- the [framework](../../../README.md)'s opening picture and its postulate formula, which read literally put the band in $`S^3`$ (§VII gives the split). Its gloss now adds that "the carrier's physical domain is $`\mathbb{RP}^3`$, the layer where it embeds" (1f). Canonical pages and tools that restate the formula carry the rewritten one; working notes keep the old shorthand as records. The [bedrock index](../../bedrock/README.md)'s Embedding statement now reads "The carrier embeds unbent in the projective layer". Its "a singular surface model, not a smooth totally geodesic submanifold of $`S^3/2I`$" holds under (A) as well as (C), since the conic carrier is singular and its completion is only immersed in $`S^3/2I`$. Since 2026-09-28 the index words its entry clause for the coexact spectrum, which "enters separately", "not by seating the Möbius band inside $`S^3/2I`$", and adds "The quotient's central stage reaches the band itself", through Lemma 1.
- the [root README](../../../../../README.md)'s opening, which now says that, once opposite points are identified, "the resulting projective three-space admits an unbent Möbius surface with a single continuous edge", and its One Interface panel, whose figure the framework's [two-seam section](../../../README.md#the-two-seams) shares.
- the [cosmos landing](../../../../cosmos/README.md)'s two sentences, which now say the surface lies unbent with opposite points identified;
- the Λ page's placement clause, in its [§II](../../../../cosmos/files/cosmological-constant.md#ii-the-geometry), which now reads "The postulate embeds a non-orientable carrier, unbent, in its central quotient", with the quotient's first stage as the layer the carrier embeds in. Its construction clause is separate: the carrier is "an embedded spectral carrier", built as "the edge-identified quotient of a totally geodesic covering great-$`S^2`$ band in $`S^3`$", as in its [§IV](../../../../cosmos/files/cosmological-constant.md#iv-the-conversion) Stage 1, the engine's seam table, the Waltz's [§II](../../../../spectrum/files/the-waltz.md#ii-gravity-as-the-cost-to-dance) table and the [topology tool](../../../../tools/files/topology.html). The construction clause can stand under (A), (A′) and (B′) if 1b selects $`M(W)`$, or another realization identified with that quotient construction, with the quotient taken abstractly under (A′) and (B′) (§I). It fails under (B), whose carrier is a different surface. Under (C) the quotient stands abstractly, as the pillar defines $`M(W)`$, and only "embedded" and "in $`S^3`$" fail. Under (B′) the placement clause holds only through the intrinsic label, since Proposition 0.2's carrier is two-sided. Since 2026-09-28 the Λ page, the seam table, the Waltz's table and the topology tool present the construction as the conic model, the leading realization, not as the carrier itself.
- the [Euclid DR1 card](../../../../cosmos/files/euclid-dr1.md)'s §I, which places the band unbent in the projective quotient, after its dated clarification of 2026-09-28 giving the rewritten formula.

On this placement the carrier is the double lune modulo $`-I`$, lying in $`\mathbb{RP}^3`$. Its preimage in $`S^3`$ is the double lune: the covering band those pages describe together with its image under $`-I`$. The band is half of it, a fundamental domain whose end arcs $`-I`$ identifies.

**The two seams.** Three pages state as structure that the Möbius embedding and the $`2I`$ quotient are different operations: the framework's [One Interface](../../../README.md#the-two-seams) ("These are different operations doing different jobs"), the root README's panel ("the framework does not treat them as interchangeable") and the [Waltz](../../../../spectrum/files/the-waltz.md#i-the-two-partners) ("The dance runs on two distinct seams"). On this placement the quotient that forms the carrier, by $`-I`$, is the central first stage of the full $`2I`$ quotient, so the two-seam picture becomes the three-layer tower $`S^3 \to \mathbb{RP}^3 \to S^3/2I`$. Since 2026-09-28 One Interface, which had called the seams separate, and the Waltz say so in place: "The two seams share one step without merging". The root README's panel says the same of its two operations: the quotient's construction "includes that same identification of opposite points as an initial step". The one coupling attempt, refuted on its route, stays refuted. The central sign acts the same way on both members of the Galois pair, spinorial for $`Q`$ and $`Q'`$ and integer-spin for their adjoints, so it cannot carry the Galois difference that route needed (INFERRED). No curvature coefficient of the Λ page or the Waltz moves: the Gauss factor $`3`$ and the $`3/2`$ conversion are local computations on the covering band, which the placement keeps. The postulate bridge's ["The deeper obstruction"](postulate-bridge.md#what-was-tried-and-why-it-failed) begins "The band lives upstairs on a great $`S^2 \subset S^3`$", and says that restricting to a great $`S^2`$ and taking $`2I`$-invariants "compose only if the surface is group-stable, and it is not". Its sampler reading repeats, unscoped, that "the band does not descend (the deeper obstruction)". The obstruction's reason, that "left translation by any non-central element moves the defining $`3`$-plane", leaves the central stage open. Under (A) the composition goes through there: the carrier's preimage, the double lune, is stable under $`\{\pm I\}`$, so the band descends to $`\mathbb{RP}^3`$, and Lemma 1 computes the result. For $`2I`$ the obstruction stands, and the bridge's scoped statement, that it "rules out descent of the band to $`X`$ as a $`2I`$-stable submanifold", stays true.

**The two signs.** These keep the Möbius sign and the central $`-I`$ apart:
- the framework's [Surface](../../../README.md#surface), [Edges sort spin](../../../README.md#edges-sort-spin) ("and not the Möbius orientation sign of One Shape") and [The Sampling Grids](../../../README.md#the-sampling-grids) ("The Möbius sign flip and the central $`-I`$ of $`2I`$ are two distinct $`Z_2`$'s");
- the postulate bridge in five places: [Route 1](postulate-bridge.md#route-1) ("the band's $`\mathbb{Z}_2`$ cannot be a $`2I`$-equivariant datum"), the opening of its [sampler reading](postulate-bridge.md#a-sampler-reading) ("the orientation $`\mathbb{Z}_2`$ is not a $`2I`$-equivariant datum"), its first bounded test ("cannot be identified with any $`2I`$-datum"), its summary of the two negatives ("$`\mathbb Z_2^{\text{Möbius}} \neq \mathbb Z_2^{\text{centre}}`$ as identified structures") and its [variational reading](postulate-bridge.md#variational-reading) ("the two $`\mathbb{Z}_2`$ structures remain distinct");
- the sampler test's [§9](sampler-first-test.md#9-what-the-negative-does-and-does-not-say) ("The central $`2I`$ sign and the Möbius normal sign are independent structures"), with its summary that both negatives separate the two as identified structures;
- the variational reading's [§3](variational-score-to-sample.md#3-phase-domain-and-möbius-sign) ("the two $`\mathbb{Z}_2`$ structures are different objects");
- the [Plato twist](plato-twist.md) ("not the Möbius orientation $`Z_2`$, which the framework keeps distinct from that split") and the [mass spectrum](../../../../spectrum/files/mass-spectrum.md#5-the-vertex-and-the-twist)'s $`\cos(\pi/10)`$ paragraph, where the Möbius $`Z_2`$ "stays distinct both from the base edge $`Z_2`$ above and from the central $`-I`$ inside the lifted $`Z_4`$".

Each holds as a statement about two different objects, or on its own route or class. On this placement the core loop is at once the Möbius orientation generator and the loop representing $`-I`$ (Lemma 0). Its orientation holonomy is $`-1`$, and the central action equals that sign exactly in the spinorial sectors, where $`\tau(-I) = -\mathrm{id}`$ (Lemma 1). Since 2026-09-28 the framework's three places and the mass spectrum's paragraph say so in place. Surface, which had left open whether the two take part in one mechanism, now opens its account with "the orientation sign and the central $`-I`$ are carried by one loop" and states §II's fence; The Sampling Grids replaces its claim that the natural sampler does not carry one sign onto the other with §II's sampler dependence. The working-layer places carry dated scope notes. The sampler test's table row, "$`\mathcal L`$'s $`\mathbb Z_2`$ cannot be a $`2I`$ character", stays true as written.

**The sampler reading.** Its structural result assumes the band has a global lift $`i : M \to S^3`$. That covers the pullback $`f^*E_\tau`$ being "canonically trivial", the preferred lift, and the boxed "transverse, not restrictive". These sit in the postulate bridge's [A sampler reading](postulate-bridge.md#a-sampler-reading), the sampler test's [§1](sampler-first-test.md#1-the-pullback-carries-no-twist), [§2](sampler-first-test.md#2-a-canonical-candidate-the-twist-is-transverse) and §9, the [working index](../README.md#the-postulate-bridge)'s summary. The bridge's opening repeats the result, leaving "the transverse-not-restrictive result as the residue". On this placement the band admits no global lift, and the core maps to $`-I`$ (§II).

The sampler test's [§7](sampler-first-test.md#7-the-obstruction) shows the stabilizer target $`\lvert S\rvert = 2`$ unreachable for one-boundary surfaces in $`S^3`$, and the working index repeats it for that class: "the deck element $`-1`$ never stabilizes an admissible band". On this placement the double lune's setwise stabilizer in $`2I`$ is $`\{\pm 1\}`$, which is $`\lvert S\rvert = 2`$, reached by a surface outside that class. The theorem excluding $`\lvert S\rvert = 2`$ does not extend to this class, but the test's verdict on the halving still holds: the lift-geometry explanation of $`120 \to 60`$ "should not be rescued by modifying the band". The reason the test attaches, §7's insensitivity to the boundary curve, does not reach this class, but its [§8](sampler-first-test.md#8-the-second-obstruction-equivariance) does, and it anticipated this case: "even had $`S = \{\pm 1\}`$ been achievable", "nothing would have been resolved at sixty either", since equivariance keeps every scalar reading identical across the translates. So the placement recovers the geometric stabilizer, not the halving mechanism.

The bridge's transfer guard asks that the band's eigenspaces "belong to a named operator on the embedded band's induced metric rather than being imported from the intrinsic conic pillar". Under (A), with $`M(W)`$ as the carrier and its operator named (§XI, 1b and 1e), the band's metric is the carrier's own induced metric on its smooth locus, not an imported model. That meets the guard's purpose. The guard was written for smooth bands in $`S^3`$, so this meets its substance, not its letter.

**Condition 2.** The linear readout's "group each block carries" ([scaling law](scaling-law-uniqueness.md#linear-readout-two-routes-and-a-no-go)) and its grade in the [claim ledger](claim-ledger.md) derive the sign $`-1`$ from the anti-periodic condition in every block. On this placement the source of the no-fixed-vector condition becomes sampler- and sector-dependent. Under the transverse sampler the integer-spin blocks carry the Möbius sign, while the spinorial blocks are untwisted and keep $`\tau(-I) = -\mathrm{id}`$ through the quotient reading that the theorem adopts. Under plain restriction the spinorial blocks carry the twisted holonomy, and the nontrivial integer-spin blocks keep Theorem A's hypothesis through irreducibility, but the real trivial block loses the $`\mathcal L`$ sign and has a fixed vector. Theorem A, stated over nonnegative invariants since 2026-09-29, needs no fixed-vector hypothesis, so condition 2's all-block proof covers this placement too.

**The cone extension.** These fix or use an extension. Every reading carries the choice, because every reading takes the Λ seed from $`M(W)`$, including (B), where a smooth carrier in $`S^3`$ has no cone of its own (§XI, 1e):
- The [scaling law](scaling-law-uniqueness.md#whats-closed) listed "the extension selection" among its open mechanisms until 1e (§XI). Its [proof](scaling-law-uniqueness.md#proof-the-schur-separation) calls Friedrichs "the framework's default" and states "This decouples the factorization from the extension selection". Under (A), with bridging and the same matching in an untwisted sector an observable reads, that decoupling fails there, since below the critical width the untwisted problem has no level at $`2/R^2`$ (§II); 1e adopts Friedrichs, so the case does not arise.
- [Cone Point Coherence](cone-point-coherence.md#i-the-question) uses the "bridging extension (not Friedrichs; see §II)" for its coherence mechanism.
- The Λ page says "the holonomy picks out the twisted sector", and in its [§III](../../../../cosmos/files/cosmological-constant.md#iii-the-spectral-seed) "The holonomy selects the twisted sector in which the first positive level is identified". Under (A) or (A′) with plain restriction (Lemma 1), and under (B′) with Proposition 0.2's carrier and either sampler (the canonical lift and its two-sidedness), the trivial block lands in the untwisted sector. So if the $`\Lambda_\text{top}`$ row is read through that block, it is not in the sector the holonomy selects (§II). Under the adopted transverse sampler (§XI, 1d) the trivial block lands in the twisted sector.

**The anti-periodic condition.** The framework's [Temporal Edge](../../../README.md#temporal-edge) states "The anti-periodic flip acts per lap", $`\psi(y + L) = -\psi(y)`$ with $`L = \pi R_\Lambda`$ on the conic model, on the one-lap lift of the core loop, while the closed two-lap edge is periodic. Condition 2's theorem derives its sign from that flip in every block. Under (A) and (A′) the flip governs the twisted sector, and which sampled blocks land there depends on the sampler, the integer-spin ones under the adopted transverse sampler (§XI, 1d); under (B′) with Proposition 0.2's carrier, no sampled block does. The untwisted class is periodic across the seam. Under Friedrichs its $`2/R^2`$ mode $`\phi_0 u_0`$ still changes sign once per lap, at the cone. Under bridging with the same matching, $`\phi_0 u_0`$ leaves the domain, and below the critical width the untwisted class has no $`2/R^2`$ mode at all (§II; §XI, 1a).

**Tier 2.** The [selection reading](postulate-bridge.md#a-dynamical-direction-candidate-tier-2s-first-computation-run)'s admissible configuration, "a circle as the edge of a Möbius band embedded in $`S^3`$", and the bar's "postulate embedding" assume the carrier sits in $`S^3`$. The bridge's opening repeats it: "Tier 2's totally geodesic candidate has been removed: it is topologically unavailable to a smooth Möbius band". The same paragraph of the selection reading says "The zero-excitation equilibrium cannot be the totally geodesic embedding", since "The topology excludes the zero-bending configuration" in $`S^3`$. The adopted premise places exactly that configuration one level down, so the admissible class changes to carriers realized through the projective layer; §IX studies the leading one, the conic band with its figure-eight edge.

**The transfer run.** Condition 1's first transfer computation, the sampler's transfer onto the band's first positive mode ([scaling law](scaling-law-uniqueness.md), Step C), has run blind ([The First-Positive Transfer Run](first-positive-transfer.md)). It took $`M(W)`$ as the declared calculational realization, with both samplers, a target operator for each class of sampler output, twisted or untwisted, and both extensions, so it decides none of 1b, 1d, 1e or 1f; its placements probed the pose, and the lowest blocks, whose outputs do not depend on it, leak at every pose. Under neither sampler does any of the eighteen ambient blocks run stay in the first-positive eigenspace, so the scaling law carries the first-positive projection as an adopted premise. Under Friedrichs the untwisted scalar problem also has a $`2/R^2`$ mode, $`\phi_0 u_0`$, discontinuous at $`p_c`$, with the same intensity (§II).

**Set aside.** The ruling set aside the bent readings, in $`\mathbb{RP}^3`$ and in $`S^3`$, the $`S^3`$ reading and the intrinsic reading; §XI keeps them, with their costs, as the decision's record. With the premise adopted, the carrier does not lie in $`S^3`$ (Lemma 0).

**Found here, independent of the ruling.** Under Friedrichs the twisted and untwisted problems have the same spectrum (§II; the pillar calls the Friedrichs realization "spectrally an orientable object"). So under every reading the Λ page's holonomy still selects the bundle's twisted sector, but under Friedrichs it does not pick out $`2/R^2`$ by separating it from the untwisted problem's spectrum. Under bridging with the same matching in the untwisted problem, $`\phi_0 u_0`$ leaves that problem's domain, and the holonomy does separate $`2/R^2`$: below the critical width that problem has no level there ([The First-Positive Transfer Run](first-positive-transfer.md#iii-the-same-cone-trace-matching), §III). So the holonomy's spectral role depends on the extension. 1e adopts Friedrichs in both sectors (§XI), where the holonomy selects the sector, not the level; the Λ page states this in its §III.
