# S³/2I and the OPH Carrier

**Type:** Note
**State:** Active
**Status (2026-10-30):** The character match is computed: as A5 representations, the two golden triplets of OPH's committed face operator are the adjoints of the holonomies of the two irreducible flat SU(2) connections on S³/2I, with W− ≅ Sym²Q (OPH's committed port frame) and W+ ≅ Sym²Q′, checked class by class in exact arithmetic against OPH commit 621edbb2. The frame degree is derived here (§III): every equivariant map from OPH's twelve-port boundary onto the Galois twin's rotation action has degree at least 7 in absolute value, the McKay distance of Q′. OPH's twin frame attains that floor. If OPH derives a source-bound identification of the local radial map with its degree-one support realization on a common oriented sphere, the degree theorem selects the convex frame over every equivariant realization of the twin, under the committed A5 action. The frame degrees and the algebraic minimum are checked by script; the floor over all continuous maps rests on the proof. OPH #1155 leaves the port-to-support attachment open, adds an endpoint-coarsening obstruction, and carries the floor as an independent proof candidate there. The Seifert rows are classical and checked against their sources. The Molien-shell work it named next has since been done in molien-shells.md (2026-09-13), whose sky step closed Uninformative (2026-09-13); FZ-02 enters neither page.
**Summary:** Where MIT's reading of S³/2I meets the twelve-port carrier of Observer Patch Holography (OPH), each framework computing in its own terms: the Seifert base and its orbifold relation against OPH's coset carrier certificate, OPH's two golden triplets against the adjoints of MIT's two flat connections, and the radial degrees of OPH's two Galois port frames against the McKay distances of those connections.
**Inputs:** `../../bedrock/files/coexact-gap.md` §4.4, `scripts/golden-sector-match.test.py`, `scripts/frame-degree/`, `molien-shells.md`, `molien-step-two.md`

[Observer Patch Holography](https://github.com/FloatingPragma/observer-patch-holography) (OPH) declares a local carrier of twelve ports with the oriented boundary complex of the icosahedron. Following the proposal in [OPH #769](https://github.com/FloatingPragma/observer-patch-holography/discussions/769), OPH merged a certificate, [OPH #784](https://github.com/FloatingPragma/observer-patch-holography/pull/784), that reconstructs the carrier as the coset geometry of a supplied $`\mathrm{SL}(2,\mathbb{F}_5)`$, conditional on that group, and its selection ledger now cites it: given the group, the carrier is derived rather than separately supplied. This note records where the carrier meets MIT's reading of $`S^3/2I`$. Each side computes in its own framework, and neither adopts the other's physical interpretation.

Classical topology and the certificate together identify the compatibility condition the certificate found by exhaustive search with the orbifold relation of the Seifert base: on the icosahedron the corners of a $`(2,3,5)`$ triangle are a vertex, the midpoint of an edge at that vertex, and the centre of a face containing both, which is the incident triple the criterion detects. They also identify the binary presentation that selects the compatible placements with the fundamental group of $`S^3/2I`$. The certificate takes the finite group as supplied and claims neither identification; both are this note's reading.

## II. The golden triplets

The [coexact gap](../../bedrock/files/coexact-gap.md) works with the two irreducible flat $`\mathrm{SU}(2)`$ connections on $`S^3/2I`$: the defining representation $`Q`$ of $`2I \subset \mathrm{SU}(2)`$ and its Galois conjugate $`Q^\prime`$. After complexifying, their adjoint representations are $`\mathrm{Sym}^2 Q`$ and $`\mathrm{Sym}^2 Q^\prime`$, the two three-dimensional irreducibles of $`A_5`$. On OPH's side, [GoldenSectorCharacters.lean](https://github.com/FloatingPragma/observer-patch-holography/blob/621edbb2a56b388b3590a5c766a59bd9d2012a17/Lean/Screen/GoldenSectorCharacters.lean) has `goldenPlusZ` and `goldenMinusZ`, twenty times the spectral projectors of the face normal operator $`N = 3I - A`$, with $`A`$ the adjacency of the twenty faces, at $`\lambda_\pm = 3 \pm \sqrt5`$. The two matrices have trace 60 (`plus_trace`, `minus_trace`), so the projectors have trace 3, and [GoldenSectorIrreducibility.lean](https://github.com/FloatingPragma/observer-patch-holography/blob/52cf7bd84eab749a3dfd220b0106c929760cf763/Lean/Screen/GoldenSectorIrreducibility.lean) proves both images rank three and irreducible (`finrank_WPlus`, `finrank_WMinus`, `goldenPlus_irreducible`, `goldenMinus_irreducible`). The images are written $`W_\pm`$ here, following the notation of [OPH #785](https://github.com/FloatingPragma/observer-patch-holography/pull/785).

Comparing characters needs a labelling of the two classes of order-five rotations that is intrinsic to each side. An order-five rotation is *one-step* when it moves each neighbour of its fixed vertex to an adjacent neighbour, and *two-step* otherwise; every graph isomorphism preserves the distinction. The one identification used comes from OPH's committed data: `PortFrameGram` places graph neighbours at inner product $`1/\sqrt5`$, the nearest-neighbour value on the unit icosahedron, and the certificate relabels the coset graph onto that same neighbour list.

| order-five rotations | port frame | $`W_-`$ | $`W_+`$ | $`\mathrm{Sym}^2 Q`$ | $`\mathrm{Sym}^2 Q^\prime`$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| one-step (12) | $`\varphi`$ | $`\varphi`$ | $`1-\varphi`$ | $`\varphi`$ | $`1-\varphi`$ |
| two-step (12) | $`1-\varphi`$ | $`1-\varphi`$ | $`\varphi`$ | $`1-\varphi`$ | $`\varphi`$ |

All five take $`3`$, $`-1`$, $`0`$ on the elements of order 1, 2, 3, so the characters agree class by class: $`\mathrm{Sym}^2 Q \cong W_-`$, the representation of the committed port frame, and $`\mathrm{Sym}^2 Q^\prime \cong W_+`$. Relative to the committed port frame, $`W_-`$ is the matching triplet and $`W_+`$ its Galois twin. This labels the twelve-and-twelve split of the order-five rotations that `GoldenSectorCharacters` records without a geometric label: $`\chi_+ = \varphi`$ exactly on the two-step class.

A check by hand needs neither the committed projectors nor quaternions. The face graph is the dodecahedron graph, and the dodecahedron vertex $`(1,1,1)`$ has neighbours $`(0, 1/\varphi, \varphi)`$, $`(1/\varphi, \varphi, 0)`$ and $`(\varphi, 0, 1/\varphi)`$, which sum to $`\sqrt5\,(1,1,1)`$ since $`\varphi + 1/\varphi = \sqrt5`$. So the coordinate triplet sits at adjacency eigenvalue $`\sqrt5`$, the eigenvalue $`3 - \sqrt5`$ of $`N`$, and a one-step rotation, through $`2\pi/5`$, has trace $`1 + 2\cos 72^\circ = \varphi`$ on it. The port frame is the coordinate triplet by construction: `PortFrameGram` proves $`G^2 = 4G`$ with trace 12, so $`G/4`$ is a rank-three projector, and its entries, fixed by graph distance, make the ports twelve icosahedral vertex directions in $`\mathbb{R}^3`$. On the $`S^3/2I`$ side a one-step rotation lifts to trace $`\pm 2\cos 36^\circ = \pm\varphi`$ in $`Q`$, and the sign drops out: $`\chi_{\mathrm{Sym}^2 Q} = \chi_Q^2 - 1 = \varphi`$, the value §4.4 of the [coexact gap](../../bedrock/files/coexact-gap.md) gives on the order-ten class.

## III. The frame degree

OPH's [Galois port-frame certificate](https://github.com/FloatingPragma/observer-patch-holography/blob/52cf7bd84eab749a3dfd220b0106c929760cf763/code/a5_closure/galois_port_frame_certificate.md) ([OPH #992](https://github.com/FloatingPragma/observer-patch-holography/pull/992)) reconstructs two Galois-conjugate rank-three frames from the committed oriented incidence: the convex frame $`G_+`$, whose radial map onto the sphere has degree 1, and its twin $`G_-`$, the great icosahedron, of degree 7. It compares the two inside that pair only. [OPH #1009](https://github.com/FloatingPragma/observer-patch-holography/pull/1009) records the conditional selector: given a source-bound attachment to OPH's degree-one support and a common oriented realization, the convex radial map lies in the degree-one class and the twin's does not. The attachment itself remains unselected there.

Write $`\rho`$ and $`\rho^\prime`$ for the rotation representations of $`A_5`$ realized by $`G_+`$ and $`G_-`$. A one-step rotation has trace $`\varphi`$ under $`\rho`$ and $`1-\varphi`$ under $`\rho^\prime`$, and the matrices of $`\rho^\prime`$ are those of $`\rho`$ with $`\sqrt5 \mapsto -\sqrt5`$ applied to every entry.

> **Proposition (derived here).** Let $`f`$ be a continuous map from OPH's oriented twelve-port boundary, with its committed action of $`A_5`$, to $`S^2`$, such that $`f(g \cdot x) = \rho^\prime(g) f(x)`$ for every $`g \in A_5`$. Then $`\deg f`$ is odd, $`\deg f \equiv \pm 2 \pmod 5`$, and $`3 \nmid \deg f`$, so $`\lvert \deg f \rvert \ge 7`$. With $`\rho`$ in place of $`\rho^\prime`$, the middle condition becomes $`\deg f \equiv \pm 1 \pmod 5`$, and degree 1 is allowed.

*Proof.* Let $`v`$ be a port and $`g`$ the one-step generator of its stabilizer that advances the link of $`v`$ positively. The rotation $`\rho^\prime(g)`$ has trace $`1 - \varphi = 1 + 2\cos(4\pi/5)`$, so it turns through $`4\pi/5`$ about an axis, and $`f(v)`$ is one of the two axis points $`\pm y`$. An involution $`h`$ in the dihedral normalizer of $`\langle g \rangle`$ sends $`v`$ to its antipodal port $`\bar v`$. Since $`h`$ normalizes $`\langle g \rangle`$, $`\rho^\prime(h)`$ preserves $`\lbrace \pm y \rbrace`$; it inverts $`\rho^\prime(g)`$, while a rotation fixing $`y`$ would commute with it, so $`\rho^\prime(h)`$ swaps the two points and $`f(\bar v) = -f(v)`$. The stabilizer of $`y`$ in $`\rho^\prime(A_5)`$ consists of rotations about its axis, so it is cyclic, and a cyclic subgroup of $`A_5`$ containing an element of order five has order five: the stabilizer is $`\rho^\prime \langle g \rangle`$. Hence no face centre or seam midpoint maps to $`y`$, and $`v`$ is the only port that does.

In charts where $`g`$ acts by $`z \mapsto e^{2\pi i/5} z`$ and $`\rho^\prime(g)`$ by $`z \mapsto e^{i\theta^\prime} z`$ with $`\theta^\prime = \pm 4\pi/5`$, no nonzero real-linear map intertwines the two, so every smooth equivariant $`f`$ has $`df_v = 0`$. A cut-off convex combination with the model $`z \mapsto z^k`$, $`k \equiv 5\theta^\prime/2\pi \pmod 5`$, carried to the other ports by equivariance, is an equivariant homotopy after which $`v`$ is isolated in $`f^{-1}(y)`$. The rest of $`f^{-1}(y)`$ then lies in a compact part of the free set, where a perturbation near one point of each orbit, carried by equivariance, makes $`f`$ transverse to $`y`$. That rest is finite and splits into free $`\langle g \rangle`$-orbits of five points with equal local degree. A small loop $`\gamma`$ around $`v`$ with $`g \cdot \gamma(t) = \gamma(t + 1/5)`$ maps to a loop winding $`5(\theta^\prime/2\pi + m)`$ times around $`y`$ for some integer $`m`$, so the local degree at $`v`$ is $`\pm 2 \pmod 5`$, and $`\deg f \equiv \pm 2 \pmod 5`$.

The same count at a face centre, where the normalizer is $`S_3`$ and $`\rho^\prime`$ of an element of order three turns through $`\pm 2\pi/3`$, gives $`3 \nmid \deg f`$. At a seam midpoint the normalizer is the Klein group, whose other involutions are half-turns about perpendicular axes and swap the two axis points, and the count gives $`\deg f`$ odd. The least positive integer meeting all three conditions is 7. For $`\rho`$ the trace is $`\varphi = 1 + 2\cos(2\pi/5)`$, the turn is $`\pm 2\pi/5`$, and the residue is $`\pm 1 \pmod 5`$. $`\square`$

Two consequences hold for OPH's committed port action:

- The floor is attained by $`G_-`$. No $`\rho^\prime`$-equivariant map of the carrier is injective, since an injective map of the sphere to itself is a homeomorphism, of degree $`\pm 1`$. The conditional selector of OPH #992 and #1009 therefore holds across every continuous equivariant realization of the twin, refined or nonlinear, and not only inside the frozen pair. Relabelling the ports by an outer automorphism of $`A_5`$ exchanges $`\rho`$ and $`\rho^\prime`$, as [OPH #1124](https://github.com/FloatingPragma/observer-patch-holography/pull/1124) records, so the statement is relative to the committed action.
- OPH #1124 finds no intertwiner from the spatial slow triplet to the internal lower response triplet, whose block is the coefficientwise Galois image of the upper block, so it carries $`\rho^\prime`$. The floor is the continuous counterpart: any equivariant map from the spatial sphere to the sphere of the lower triplet has degree at least 7 in absolute value, and none is injective.

The floor meets the McKay distances. A homogeneous polynomial map $`F : \mathbb{C}^2 \to \mathbb{C}^2`$ of degree $`a`$ with $`F(Q(g)z) = Q^\prime(g)F(z)`$ for all $`g \in 2I`$ exists exactly when $`Q^\prime \subset \mathrm{Sym}^a Q`$, first at $`a = d(Q^\prime) = 7`$ ([coexact gap](../../bedrock/files/coexact-gap.md), Lemma 4.1), with a one-dimensional space there. Because $`2I`$ is perfect, every equivariant rational map from the $`\rho`$-sphere to the $`\rho^\prime`$-sphere lifts to such an $`F`$, and the central element then forces $`a`$ odd. The two components of $`F`$ share no zero: a common zero set would be a union of icosahedral orbits, each of at least 12 points, while neither component vanishes identically, since the other's at most seven zeros would then form a nonempty invariant set, so the two share at most seven. So the map has degree exactly 7. It is the icosahedrally symmetric degree-7 map of Houghton, Manton and Sutcliffe, eq. (3.18), which their $`B = 7`$ Skyrmion uses in another orientation ((3.19) at $`b = \pm 7/\sqrt5`$); by their §4 and (4.6) its numerator and denominator span $`E_2^\prime`$, which is $`Q^\prime`$, the only doublet in $`\mathrm{Sym}^7 Q`$.

Kostant's generating function for $`Q^\prime`$ in the symmetric powers has numerator $`t^7 + t^{13} + t^{17} + t^{23}`$ over $`(1 - t^{12})(1 - t^{20})`$, so its generators sit exactly at the residues mod 30 the proposition allows. For $`Q`$ the numerator is $`t + t^{11} + t^{19} + t^{29}`$, matching the degrees of the icosahedral maps below degree 31 that Doyle and McMullen classify. The least algebraic and the least topological degrees therefore agree here, $`1 = d(Q)`$ and $`7 = d(Q^\prime)`$; no general statement is claimed. The congruences and the topological floor were not located in print in the searches made. They follow from standard equivariant-degree arguments, so they are a check rather than a new result.

The degree difference, 6, is rational and nonzero, which the Galois-parity argument on [OPH #786](https://github.com/FloatingPragma/observer-patch-holography/discussions/786#discussioncomment-18437090) says needs a second input that conjugation also moves. Read this way, that input is the choice of real embedding: the sign of $`\sqrt5`$ fixes the signs of the face determinants and which branch is convex, and OPH #992 checks Gram positivity at each embedding separately. The ambient orientation is a separate input, fixed for both frames, and reversing it flips both degrees together. The degree explains how the branches can differ by an integer; it supplies no independent physical choice of embedding.

OPH has taken this up in its own terms. The [maintainer's CP-13 note](https://github.com/FloatingPragma/observer-patch-holography/issues/1137#issuecomment-6485613923) asks first whether any active requirement needs an injective or degree-one identification at all, since distinct spatial and internal factors remain an admissible construction; if none does, the result is kept as a conditional constraint. It also records that the port weights of [OPH #1150](https://github.com/FloatingPragma/observer-patch-holography/pull/1150) are not invariant under the full action, so applying the proposition there needs its own hypothesis map. [OPH #1155](https://github.com/FloatingPragma/observer-patch-holography/pull/1155) adds an [independent proof candidate](https://github.com/FloatingPragma/observer-patch-holography/blob/dd7eff26f5fcb94d45ab6f2bc97d615f10fe0358/code/a5_closure/continuous_equivariant_degree_proof.md) that reaches the same residues mod 30 through a cyclic local-index congruence and normalizer pole separation, and its executable controls recover Kostant's numerators. In OPH's record the continuous statement stays a proof candidate: those controls check finite arithmetic and representation typing, and its review of the approximation, transversality and normalizer steps is outstanding there.

MIT reads the twin as the frame of $`\mathrm{Sym}^2 Q^\prime`$, the adjoint that carries the $`36/R^2`$ exception of the [coexact gap](../../bedrock/files/coexact-gap.md). That is a reading: the degree and the gap are different quantities, and no operator joins them.

## IV. Limits

- The same Galois-conjugate triplet is higher in both constructions: $`36/R^2`$ against $`4/R^2`$ here, $`3 + \sqrt5`$ against $`3 - \sqrt5`$ in OPH. MIT's numbers are squared first-occurrence levels in the branching of $`2I`$: $`\mathrm{Sym}^2 Q`$ first occurs at level two and $`\mathrm{Sym}^2 Q^\prime`$ not until level six ([coexact gap](../../bedrock/files/coexact-gap.md), Theorem 4.2 and §4.4). OPH's are eigenvalues of a face operator on a twenty-dimensional space. The quantities are of different kinds and the ratios differ, $`9`$ against $`\varphi^4`$, so no shared mechanism is claimed.
- Both adjoints are trivial on the centre, so the match of §II lives on $`A_5`$, and the coset certificate cannot see the centre. OPH has since committed a faithful determinant-one doublet, the counterpart of $`Q`$ ([OPH #788](https://github.com/FloatingPragma/observer-patch-holography/pull/788), conditional on its declared current fixture), with its affine $`E_8`$ McKay graph ([OPH #790](https://github.com/FloatingPragma/observer-patch-holography/pull/790)). The frame degree of §III uses only $`A_5`$ acting on $`S^2`$; the doublet distances enter there through the algebraic argument.
- Nothing here selects $`2I`$, a real embedding of $`\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt5)`$ or OPH's support attachment, or bears on OPH's selection ledger.

If OPH derives a degree-one port-to-support attachment, §III says it selects $`G_+`$, the frame of $`\mathrm{Sym}^2 Q`$, over every equivariant realization of the twin.
- Selection: why $`2I`$ among the finite subgroups of $`\mathrm{SU}(2)`$. Parked on both sides.
