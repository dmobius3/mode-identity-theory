## I. The Question

Can one minimal, local, covariant action, common to the eight slot fields, be written on the fixed static arena without using any target behaviour? Target behaviour means any outcome on OpenWave's [M8.2 success ladder](https://github.com/openwave-labs/openwave/blob/6e330a6f49141bc77f926d48e62a02093358b343/openwave/xperiments/m8_mit/research/findings/m8_2_preregistration.md#L168-L181): an admissible background, a stable vacuum, a common law that works, sector sensitivity, stable nonlinear branches, or a relation among slot energies. No such outcome may choose the quadratic operator, the self-interaction, a sign or a coupling ratio. If such an action exists it is frozen here, before any run; if not, the page closes OPEN with the free element named.

**Scope.** Eight independent complex fields, one per nontrivial slot, with the trivial bundle as a control. Two other field contents are deferred to separate registrations and play no part here: one doublet whose covariants give the slot fields ([the slot relation's recorded item](slot-relation.md#viii-report)), and one complex field on the cover, whose components fill every slot at once. Either would need its own action, frozen under the same discipline.

**No relation is scored.** [The slot relation](slot-relation.md) is reported OPEN: MIT files no relation among slot energies, so energies computed under this action are outputs, compared with no prediction.

## II. What the Object Is

- **Fields.** For each irreducible representation ρ of 2I, ψ_ρ is a complex section of the flat bundle $`E_\rho = S^3 \times_{2I} V_\rho`$ over $`S^3/2I`$ ([§II of the slot relation](slot-relation.md#ii-the-slots)), with 2I acting on S³ by left translation; the Surviving Ray's right-translation convention is the same structure under inversion. The eight nontrivial representations $`R_1, \ldots, R_8`$ are the slots. $`R_0`$, whose sections are functions on S³/2I, is the control.
- **Type.** The fields are spacetime scalars carrying an internal 2I label: an effective complex slot-amplitude field theory on the observable quotient. In OpenWave's terms it is an M8-owned twisted extension, not a native family ([M8.4 pre-registration](https://github.com/openwave-labs/openwave/blob/6e330a6f49141bc77f926d48e62a02093358b343/openwave/xperiments/m8_mit/research/findings/m8_4_preregistration.md#L27-L29)).
- **Not physical matter.** It is not MIT's physical matter action: MIT leaves the Standard Model's particle content unchanged ([the framework](../../../README.md)). The four spinorial slots gain no Lorentz spinor index and no fermionic statistics; their label is the central $`-I`$'s action, and nothing here derives spin-statistics.
- **Not 𝒮_MIT.** It couples no wave-and-budget sector, embedding, sampler or clock. It is not the action both bridges wait on ([Variational Score-to-Sample](variational-score-to-sample.md#16-parked)), and that page's admission bar, written for the clock's potential, does not govern it; [the bar](postulate-bridge.md#dynamical-direction)'s independence clause does (§III).
- **Its stress tensor** is defined by varying the covariant action with respect to the metric. It belongs to the extension: it is not fed back to the metric, and it is not the tensor [the stress-tensor bridge](stress-tensor-bridge.md) is missing.
- **Arena.** $`\mathbb{R} \times S^3/2I`$ with the static metric $`ds^2 = -c^2\,dT^2 + R^2\,d\Omega_3^2`$, R the curvature radius, held fixed: test fields, no backreaction. The Möbius edge stays out. S³/2I is the observable domain, "where the sampler's target bundles live" ([the carrier ruling](projective-carrier.md#xi-the-ruling), 1f).

- **Slot typing.** Slot fields are sections of the $`E_\rho`$ ([§III of the slot relation](slot-relation.md#iii-the-object); C11 of [the stress-tensor bridge](stress-tensor-bridge.md)).
- **One common law, no per-slot constant** ([§III of the slot relation](slot-relation.md#iii-the-object); M8.4's [anti-tuning rule](https://github.com/openwave-labs/openwave/blob/6e330a6f49141bc77f926d48e62a02093358b343/openwave/xperiments/m8_mit/research/findings/m8_4_preregistration.md#L223-L227)).
- **The free ladder earns nothing** ([§III item 4 of the slot relation](slot-relation.md#iii-the-object); the [M8.4 kinematic close](https://github.com/openwave-labs/openwave/blob/6e330a6f49141bc77f926d48e62a02093358b343/openwave/xperiments/m8_mit/research/findings/m8_4_kinematic_close.md#L112-L116)).
- **The pointwise law derived and pinned here** (OpenWave [discussion #512](https://github.com/openwave-labs/openwave/discussions/512#discussioncomment-18415036)).

Disciplines adopted for this page from narrower sources:
- **The freeze rule.** "the kinetic term and the potential must each be read from a named corpus object ... and frozen in a commit before the run" ([Variational Score-to-Sample](variational-score-to-sample.md), §11, written for that program's run). Here the named objects are the slot sector's.
- **No new scale** ([the bar](postulate-bridge.md#dynamical-direction), written for the embedding functional; [§III of the slot relation](slot-relation.md#iii-the-object), for the relation's realizations).
- **Minimality.** No term beyond what the named objects carry, and no structure beyond what the fields and their kinetic term already require, unless it is independently motivated.
- **Admissibility.** Locality, general covariance and an energy bounded below: standard conditions on a classical field theory, not outcomes on the ladder.

## IV. Selection

1. **Field content: adopted.** One complex field ψ_ρ for each ρ in $`R_0, \ldots, R_8`$, and no term coupling two sectors. Complex fields are forced by the four quaternionic slots ([M8.4 pre-registration](https://github.com/openwave-labs/openwave/blob/6e330a6f49141bc77f926d48e62a02093358b343/openwave/xperiments/m8_mit/research/findings/m8_4_preregistration.md#L31-L41)). The named self-interaction is single-sector, the Surviving Ray stating "The mixed problem is not posed here" (§9.1), and slot identity is bundle identity ([M8.4 pre-registration](https://github.com/openwave-labs/openwave/blob/6e330a6f49141bc77f926d48e62a02093358b343/openwave/xperiments/m8_mit/research/findings/m8_4_preregistration.md#L132-L135)); minimality adds no coupling between sectors.
2. **Time: an adopted convention.** The action is written in the Killing time T of the static metric, and the lifted phase parameter t of the slot relation's time convention ([§III](slot-relation.md#iii-the-object)) is aligned with it, $`t = \kappa T + t_0`$ with κ > 0 not fixed. The convention assigns the 4π cycle no duration and says nothing about $`\tau_H`$. It is inert here, since the action never sees t; it becomes physical only if a coupling to Ψ(t) or to the clock is added.
3. **Quadratic operator: read from a named object.** The wave operator of the Laplacian on sections of $`E_\rho`$, whose levels are $`k(k+2)/R^2`$ with the lowest at $`k = \mathrm{dist}(\rho)`$ ([§II of the slot relation](slot-relation.md#ii-the-slots); the Coexact Gap paper's twisted scalar levels).
4. **Curvature coupling: ξ = 0, MOTIVATED.** A scale-free action leaves a dimensionless coupling $`\xi\,\mathcal{R}\,\lvert\psi\rvert^2`$ to the scalar curvature free; renormalizability requires such a term and leaves ξ "an arbitrary, free parameter" (Brown and Collins 1980). This page takes ξ = 0, because the named slot operator carries no curvature term and minimality adds none. Carrying that operator into the covariant action with no curvature term is minimal coupling, which gives ξ = 0. By contrast, Sonego and Faraoni (1993) derive ξ = 1/6 "by applying the equivalence principle to the propagation of scalar waves", and ξ = 1/6 is also the value at which the action is Weyl invariant. The corpus has adopted neither Weyl invariance nor a reading of the principle for matter fields, so this choice sits on one side of a published disagreement without the corpus adopting that side; an adopted Weyl invariance, or a reading of the principle that fixes ξ = 1/6, would overturn it (§VIII). On this arena ξ shifts every level by $`6\xi/R^2`$, so no slot difference depends on it at zero amplitude. At finite amplitude it matters: ξ = 1/6 puts the frequencies on the resonant lattice $`\omega = c(k+1)/R`$ and ξ = 0 does not, so row 6's results are results for ξ = 0 (§VII).
5. **Mass: none, adopted.** The named operator carries none, and a mass from another scale would need its own motivation (no new scale). On this arena a common $`m^2 \propto R^{-2}`$ is the same as a shift of ξ.
6. **Self-interaction: MOTIVATED by minimal invariant structure.** The field definition and the kinetic term already require the invariant Hermitian fibre metric $`h_\rho`$. Among local quartic potentials common to every slot and built from that structure alone, the density quartic $`(g_4/4)\lvert\psi\rvert^4 = (g_4/4)(\psi^{\dagger}\psi)^2`$, evaluated pointwise, is unique up to its common coefficient. It is the Surviving Ray's density-type interaction ([§2.5](../../bedrock/files/surviving-ray.md)), fixed there for its channel-selection question at level 6 and called in the paper's opening "a hypothesis and not a consequence"; that status travels with it. A second uniform quartic, $`\lvert\psi^{T}C_\rho\psi\rvert^2`$ with $`C_\rho`$ the invariant bilinear form, exists, but it brings in that additional structure and distinguishes the real slots from the quaternionic ones, so minimality leaves it out. Quartics specific to one sector would break the common law.
7. **Sign: g₄ > 0, admissibility.** With g₄ < 0 the energy is unbounded below. g₄ > 0 matches the Surviving Ray's normalisation, g = 1, whose energy carries "a positive multiple of g∫|ψ|⁴" (§6.2).
8. **Magnitude: g₄ = 1, a classical dynamical normalization.** For any common g₄ > 0, the rescaling $`\phi = \sqrt{g_4}\,\psi`$ maps the field equation to the g₄ = 1 equation, while the action, energy, charge and stress tensor acquire an overall factor $`1/g_4`$. So g₄ does not affect the classical existence and stability structure this page is built to test, and it is set to 1 for that purpose, as the Surviving Ray sets its own g (§2.5). Absolute energies, the normalization of $`T_{\mu\nu}`$, scattering strength or quantization would make g₄ physical again and need a separately motivated value.
9. **Normalizations: definitions.** The representations are unitary, the fibre metric $`h_\rho`$ is the identity, and the kinetic terms are canonical in it, so that one coefficient common to the sectors has a meaning.
10. **Classical: adopted scope.** Quantization is a separate choice, with vacuum energies that can depend on the twist; the N = 4 supersymmetric Casimir energy of Choi and Tachikawa (2026) is a case that does not.

**Result.** Every element is fixed by a named corpus object, minimal invariant structure, an admissibility condition, a normalization, a definition or a labeled adoption, and none by an outcome on the ladder. The question of §I is answered yes. The selection is MOTIVATED, not derived, and the action is frozen in §V.

## V. The Frozen Action

For each ρ in $`R_0, R_1, \ldots, R_8`$, independently, the frozen functional of a metric $`g_{\mu\nu}`$ and a section ψ of $`E_\rho`$ over $`\mathbb{R} \times X`$, $`X = S^3/2I`$, is

```math
\mathcal{S}_\rho[g_{\mu\nu},\psi] = -\frac{1}{2c}\int_{\mathbb{R}\times X} d^4x\,\sqrt{-g}\,\left[ g^{\mu\nu}\,(D_\mu\psi)^{\dagger} D_\nu\psi + \frac{g_4}{2}\,\lvert\psi\rvert^4 \right], \qquad g_4 = 1 .
```

Here D is the covariant derivative of the flat connection on $`E_\rho`$, pulled back to $`\mathbb{R} \times X`$, and |·| is the norm of the invariant Hermitian form. For these scalar sections the metric enters only through $`g^{\mu\nu}`$ and $`\sqrt{-g}`$: there is no curvature term and no mass. The stress tensor is $`T_{\mu\nu} = -(2c/\sqrt{-g})\,\delta\mathcal{S}_\rho/\delta g^{\mu\nu}`$, evaluated on the frozen metric (§II); the factor c undoes the functional's 1/c, so that for the static observer $`T_{\mu\nu}u^\mu u^\nu`$ is the integrand of $`E_\rho`$ below. On the frozen static product metric $`ds^2 = -c^2\,dT^2 + R^2\,d\Omega_3^2`$ the functional reduces to

```math
\mathcal{S}_\rho[\psi] = \int dT \int_{X} dV \left[ \frac{1}{2c^2}\,\lvert\partial_T\psi\rvert^2 - \frac{1}{2}\,\lvert\nabla\psi\rvert^2 - \frac{g_4}{4}\,\lvert\psi\rvert^4 \right],
```

with ∇ the flat connection's covariant derivative over the round X of radius R and dV the round volume.

The field equation, with gradients taken in the underlying real Hilbert metric, is

```math
\frac{1}{c^2}\,\partial_T^2\psi \;-\; \Delta\psi \;+\; g_4\,\lvert\psi\rvert^2\psi \;=\; 0 ,
```

with Δ the Laplacian on sections of $`E_\rho`$, so that $`-\Delta`$ has levels $`k(k+2)/R^2`$, $`k \ge \mathrm{dist}(\rho)`$.

**The law, pinned.** The nonlinear term is evaluated pointwise, $`\lvert\psi(x)\rvert^2\psi(x)`$ at each point x of S³/2I. The global form, with the spatial integral $`\langle\psi,\psi\rangle`$ in place of the pointwise norm, is a different law ([OpenWave PR #508](https://github.com/openwave-labs/openwave/pull/508#issuecomment-5499159237)).

**Standing waves.** Substituting $`\psi = e^{i\omega T}\varphi`$ gives

```math
\left(-\Delta - \frac{\omega^2}{c^2}\right)\varphi \;+\; g_4\,\lvert\varphi\rvert^2\varphi \;=\; 0 ,
```

the Surviving Ray's §2.5 equation with its λ equal to $`\omega^2/c^2`$, and OpenWave M8.10's reduction ([M8.10 task](https://github.com/openwave-labs/openwave/blob/6e330a6f49141bc77f926d48e62a02093358b343/openwave/xperiments/m8_mit/research/tasks/m8_10_task_details.md#L46-L54)).

**Conserved quantities.** The static metric's Killing field, the phase symmetry and the right translations, which are the isometries of S³/2I, give the energy and charge

```math
E_\rho = \int dV \left[ \frac{1}{2c^2}\,\lvert\partial_T\psi\rvert^2 + \frac{1}{2}\,\lvert\nabla\psi\rvert^2 + \frac{g_4}{4}\,\lvert\psi\rvert^4 \right],
\qquad
N_\rho = \frac{1}{c^2}\int dV\; \mathrm{Im}\left(\psi^{\dagger}\,\partial_T\psi\right),
```

and three conserved right-SU(2) Noether charges. On a standing wave the energy density and the whole stress tensor are time-independent, which a real oscillating field's are not.

**A shared law, stated.** This is the law OpenWave's M8 line used: the engine's cubic extended to Hermitian fields, which M8.10 identifies with the Surviving Ray's §2.5 equation, with the restoring sign M8.5-C pinned (M8.10 to M8.13 state their values for both signs). The shared lineage is not among §IV's reasons; it is why the Surviving Ray's level-6 results, and M8.10 to M8.13's, already hold for this action (§VII).

## VI. Decided Without a Run

These follow from the frozen action alone (DERIVED; no numerics):

1. **The free spectrum** is the ladder: $`\omega^2/c^2 = k(k+2)/R^2`$ on $`E_\rho`$, $`k \ge \mathrm{dist}(\rho)`$. It earns nothing (§III). In the $`R_0`$ control the constant mode, k = 0, has ω = 0.
2. **Row 2.** The zero section is a global section of every $`E_\rho`$, a constant the holonomy fixes, so an admissible background exists in all eight sectors ([Precondition B](https://github.com/openwave-labs/openwave/blob/6e330a6f49141bc77f926d48e62a02093358b343/openwave/xperiments/m8_mit/research/findings/m8_2_preregistration.md#L112-L120)).
3. **Row 3.** About the zero section the linearization is the free equation. On every nontrivial $`E_\rho`$, $`-\Delta \ge \mathrm{dist}(\rho)(\mathrm{dist}(\rho)+2)/R^2 \ge 3/R^2 > 0`$, so no mode grows: the zero background is linearly stable in all eight sectors, the Lorentzian clause of [the bar](postulate-bridge.md#dynamical-direction). The energy is conserved and, on a nontrivial $`E_\rho`$, bounded below by $`\tfrac12\int\left(\lvert\partial_T\psi\rvert^2/c^2 + (3/R^2)\lvert\psi\rvert^2\right)dV`$, so the zero section is also Lyapunov stable in the energy norm for as long as a solution exists. In the control the zero section is marginal at the constant mode.
4. **Row 4, at the background.** One frozen law gives items 2 and 3 in all eight sectors with no sector-specific choice. Whether the same law gives stable nonlinear branches in all eight is row 6's question.
5. **Row 5** is not evaluable: no term depends on a flat connection beyond the bundle's own ([M8.4 pre-registration](https://github.com/openwave-labs/openwave/blob/6e330a6f49141bc77f926d48e62a02093358b343/openwave/xperiments/m8_mit/research/findings/m8_4_preregistration.md#L139-L149)).
6. **Row 6, lowest level.** For $`R_1, R_3, R_6, R_7`$ and $`R_8`$ the restriction of $`\mathrm{Sym}^{\mathrm{dist}}\mathbb{C}^2`$ to 2I is the slot itself, so every lowest-level section is $`\psi_v(x) = \pi(x/R)\,v`$ with π the unitary representation of SU(2) on $`\mathrm{Sym}^{\mathrm{dist}}\mathbb{C}^2`$, and $`\lvert\psi_v\rvert = \lvert v\rvert`$ everywhere. So $`\psi = e^{i\omega T}\psi_v`$ solves the field equation exactly for every v, with $`\omega^2/c^2 = \mathrm{dist}(\mathrm{dist}+2)/R^2 + g_4\lvert v\rvert^2`$: a profile-preserving nonlinear branch, the exact form, for this law, of the Surviving Ray's Corollary 5.4. By M8.4's pre-label it is "nonlinear persistence of the installed free structure" and earns nothing. For $`R_2, R_4`$ and $`R_5`$ the restriction is reducible, the lowest sections' norm varies over S³, and the pointwise law leaks out of the level. The stability of any branch, in any slot, is not decided here.
7. **Localized states.** A localized stationary state $`e^{i\omega T}\varphi`$ of this law in flat three-space vanishes, by the two identities displayed after this list. Static lumps fall to Derrick's argument (Derrick 1964). At scales much smaller than R the arena is flat to $`O(L^2/R^2)`$, so no localized lump is expected here (INFERRED).

For item 7: multiplying the reduced equation by $`\bar\varphi`$ and integrating over flat three-space, and the Pohozaev identity in three dimensions, give

```math
\int\lvert\nabla\varphi\rvert^2 = \frac{\omega^2}{c^2}\int\lvert\varphi\rvert^2 - g_4\int\lvert\varphi\rvert^4,
\qquad
\frac12\int\lvert\nabla\varphi\rvert^2 = \frac{3\omega^2}{2c^2}\int\lvert\varphi\rvert^2 - \frac{3g_4}{4}\int\lvert\varphi\rvert^4,
```

whence $`\int\lvert\nabla\varphi\rvert^2 = -3(\omega^2/c^2)\int\lvert\varphi\rvert^2 \le 0`$, and φ = 0. The conclusion uses the massless reduced quadratic coefficient, $`-\omega^2/c^2 \le 0`$; a mass term would replace it by $`m^2 - \omega^2/c^2`$ and close this route. For the massless equation the conclusion holds for either sign of g₄.

## VIII. What Would Change the Selection

Each of these would be a new selection, frozen afresh on its own page; nothing above is edited:
- an adopted Weyl invariance for matter fields, or an adopted reading of the equivalence principle that fixes the curvature coupling, which would move ξ to 1/6;
- a corpus object adopting the second uniform quartic, or a quartic specific to one sector;
- a motivated term coupling two sectors (the Surviving Ray's unposed mixed problem), or one that depends on a flat connection σ;
- a motivated mass built from a scale the framework already admits;
- a coupling to Ψ(t) or to the clock, which would also make the time convention physical;
- a claim that makes g₄ physical, such as an absolute energy, the normalization of $`T_{\mu\nu}`$, a scattering strength or quantization, which would need a separately motivated value (§IV item 8).

## IX. Notes

**Note (2026-10-04), on the fibre symmetry.** Sections I to VIII stand as frozen. The frozen action has more internal symmetry than §V records, and §VII's stability protocol has to account for it.

- **Charge conjugation, in every slot.** Every irreducible representation of 2I has a real character, so $`\bar\rho \cong \rho`$: a unitary $`C`$ satisfies $`C\,\bar\rho(\gamma) = \rho(\gamma)\,C`$ for every $`\gamma \in 2I`$. The map $`\psi \mapsto C\bar\psi`$ is antilinear, commutes with $`\rho(2I)`$ and with the flat connection, and preserves $`\lvert\psi\rvert`$ pointwise, so it is a symmetry of the frozen action. It reverses the sign of $`N_\rho`$.
- **The real slots and the control,** $`R_0, R_3, R_4, R_5`$ and $`R_7`$. Here $`C\bar C = 1`$. Charge conjugation is a discrete $`\mathbb{Z}_2`$, and with the phase the fibre symmetry is $`O(2) = U(1) \rtimes \mathbb{Z}_2`$. It adds no conserved charge.
- **The quaternionic slots,** $`R_1, R_2, R_6`$ and $`R_8`$. Here $`C\bar C = -1`$, so $`J\psi = C\bar\psi`$ is a quaternionic structure, $`J^2 = -1`$. Then $`C`$ is antisymmetric, so $`\psi^{\dagger}J\psi = 0`$. With the phase, $`J`$ generates the group of maps $`a + bJ`$ with $`\lvert a\rvert^2 + \lvert b\rvert^2 = 1`$, which is $`Sp(1)`$ and preserves $`\lvert\psi\rvert`$ pointwise. It is continuous, and it adds two conserved charges to §V's.

In a quaternionic slot, with $`\mathrm{Re}\langle a, b\rangle = \mathrm{Re}(a^{\dagger}b)`$, the charge of a generator $`\xi`$ is

```math
N_\xi = \frac{1}{c^2}\int dV\; \mathrm{Re}\langle \partial_T\psi,\, \xi\psi\rangle, \qquad \xi \in \lbrace i,\ J,\ iJ \rbrace,
```

and $`\xi = i`$ gives §V's $`N_\rho`$. Each is conserved because $`\xi`$ is orthogonal with $`\xi^2 = -1`$, commutes with the covariant derivative, and preserves $`\lvert\psi\rvert`$ pointwise.

**What it changes for §VII.** The protocol removes "the exact phase and right-translation symmetry modes". That phrase is not an exhaustive prescription.
- **In every slot,** a stability claim must state the orbit or branch family relative to which it is made. It must account for every exact neutral direction, whether a symmetry or a family of exact branches generates it, and declare any remaining kernel separately. One such family follows from §VI item 6: every $`v'`$ with $`\lvert v'\rvert = \lvert v\rvert`$ gives an exact lowest-level branch of the same frequency, so in $`R_3, R_6, R_7`$ and $`R_8`$ that family is larger than any symmetry orbit.
- **In the real slots,** charge conjugation is discrete: it maps critical orbits to critical orbits and adds no continuous direction.
- **In the quaternionic slots,** the full $`Sp(1)`$-generated stationary family must be included in that accounting. Its directions are tangent to the stationary critical family, because the stationary equation is $`Sp(1)`$-equivariant. But a fixed standing wave selects one phase generator, and the other generators do not commute with it. So one $`Sp(1)`$ family holds distinct standing waves, and their spectral stability need not agree: it must be assessed point by point along the family, not inferred from the symmetry of the stationary equation.

**What it does not change.** The selection, the action, the field equation, §VI's results and §VII's ceilings stand. The symmetry was in §V's action from the start; §V did not record it.

**Note (2026-10-04), on stability.** Sections I to VIII stand as frozen. [The Slot Ground States](slot-ground-states.md) proves, without a run, that the exact lowest-level families of §VI item 6 are orbitally stable as sets at every nonzero charge, and that every slot has an orbitally stable set of fixed-charge ground states. For those families this supplies §VII's orbital-stability alternative. Their label stays M8.4's "nonlinear persistence of the installed free structure", and on OpenWave the rung is a filing's to adjudicate. Whether the ground-state sets of $`R_2, R_4`$ and $`R_5`$ form branches, and the stability of non-minimizing branches, remain open.

**Note (2026-10-05), on the linear spectrum.** Sections I to VIII stand as frozen. [The Slot Ground States](slot-ground-states.md) (Theorem D) now records, at every member of the exact lowest-level families of §VI item 6, the linear spectral stability §VII asks for first: the linearization's whole spectrum lies on the imaginary axis, and its kernel is exactly the tangent to the family at fixed charge. With the orbital-stability theorem of the note above, both parts of §VII's stability protocol are supplied for those families, under the label and the OpenWave ceiling stated there.

**Note (2026-10-06), on the soft slots' branches.** Sections I to VIII stand as frozen. [The Soft-Slot Branches](soft-slot-branches.md) proves, without a run, that each soft slot carries a branch of standing waves at small amplitude through a nondegenerate minimum of its reduced quartic, spectrally and orbitally stable relative to its stationary family, which in $`R_2`$ includes the $`Sp(1)`$ family the first note above requires. For those branches this supplies both parts of §VII's stability protocol at small amplitude, under M8.4's label "nonlinear persistence of the installed free structure"; on OpenWave the rung is a filing's to adjudicate. At the reduced quartic's other nondegenerate critical orbits it decides the small-amplitude spectral type. Everything at finite amplitude stays open.

**Note (2026-10-09), on the second quartic.** Sections I to VIII stand as frozen. §IV item 6 calls $`\lvert\psi^{T}C_\rho\psi\rvert^2`$ a second uniform quartic, and §VIII repeats the phrase; it is not uniform. In the quaternionic slots $`C_\rho`$ is antisymmetric. The note of 2026-10-04 on the fibre symmetry shows that its unitary $`C`$ is antisymmetric there, and conjugating that note's condition $`C\,\bar\rho(\gamma) = \rho(\gamma)\,C`$ gives $`\rho(\gamma)^{T}\,\bar C\,\rho(\gamma) = \bar C`$ for unitary $`\rho`$, so the invariant bilinear form $`C_\rho`$ of §IV item 6 is a multiple of $`\bar C`$ (Schur's lemma) and antisymmetric with it. Hence $`\psi^{T}C_\rho\psi`$, a scalar equal to its own transpose $`\psi^{T}C_\rho^{T}\psi = -\psi^{T}C_\rho\psi`$, vanishes identically. The quartic is therefore zero in $`R_1, R_2, R_6`$ and $`R_8`$ and a genuine quartic only in the real slots and the control (DERIVED). It separates the two kinds of slot by vanishing on one of them, so it was never a candidate for a law common to all eight, and its exclusion stands with one more reason than §IV item 6 gives.

**Note (2026-10-09), on a mass and localized states.** Sections I to VIII stand as frozen. The remark after §VI item 7 says that a mass term would close its route to $`\varphi = 0`$. For the frozen sign $`g_4 > 0`$ the conclusion survives a mass. With $`\mu = m^2 - \omega^2/c^2`$ in place of $`-\omega^2/c^2`$, the two identities combine to $`\mu\int\lvert\varphi\rvert^2 + (g_4/4)\int\lvert\varphi\rvert^4 = 0`$ and $`\int\lvert\nabla\varphi\rvert^2 = 3\mu\int\lvert\varphi\rvert^2`$. For $`g_4 > 0`$ the first forces $`\varphi = 0`$ when $`\mu \ge 0`$ and the second when $`\mu < 0`$, so in flat three-space, the setting of §VI item 7, localized standing waves stay excluded at every $`\mu`$; a mass can admit them only for $`g_4 < 0`$, the sign §IV item 7 excludes (DERIVED). The same holds for §VIII's motivated mass: it would be a new selection, and with $`g_4 > 0`$ it would leave §VI item 7's conclusion, and §VII's particle-stability row, as they stand.
